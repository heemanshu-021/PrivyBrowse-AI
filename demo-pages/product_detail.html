Pragyan Lunar Rover Module

Autonomous 26-kilogram lunar surface rover designed and built by ISRO for the Chandrayaan-3 mission. Operated successfully on the lunar south pole region at 69.37°S, 32.35°E.

Overview & Mission Architecture

The rover chassis employs a rocker-bogie suspension system with six independently driven wheels. Solar arrays deliver up to 50 watts of electrical power. Scroll down to inspect detailed telemetry specifications.
